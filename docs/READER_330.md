# Motero 1.1.4 / Build 330

- The initial phone/tablet navigation choice is saved across rotation. Responsive content still adapts, but changing available dimensions cannot replace the active NavHost. Opening a Reader consults that controller's backstack instead of a process-global flag; a disposed graph cannot prevent every subsequent document from opening.
- Protect reader intercepts hardware Back and Escape while the Reader is resumed. Repeats trigger once and paired key releases are consumed. IME and modal settings keep standard navigation. Android BackHandler/gesture exclusions and the explicit toolbar exit remain separate. This covers standard Android key events; proprietary BOOX firmware behavior still requires device confirmation.
- EPUB appearance offers original/left/justified/center/right alignment and a boldness slider. A small inherited text stroke increases weight without replacing embedded fonts or flattening existing bold/italic distinctions. The unused fraction of a text line is split above/below the paginated frame. Full-bleed margins, fixed-layout books, vertical writing and scrolled layout keep their behavior; original paragraph/title spacing is preserved.
- Wider docks expose a separate mirrored Zotero Undo icon as Redo, retaining long-press Redo on Undo. The dock and palette expand enough to show every existing drawing color when space permits. Narrow screens retain horizontal scrolling.
- Repeatable pen widths use 0.1 increments from 0.5 to 1, 0.25 to 3, 0.5 to 6, 1 to 12 and 2 to 24, ending at 25. Slider positions and persistence share those presets. Existing strokes and saved widths are not rewritten until the user changes the setting.
- Pen contacts on the PDF/EPUB quick palette belong to the controls and do not reset the selection or start document gestures. Mouse, finger, pen and eraser ownership remain separate.
- A lightweight sibling frame casts the PDF crop shadow outside the clipped page. Only prepared frames have a DOM border/shadow; no extra raster/canvas is allocated. Continuous keeps a 24 CSS-pixel separator, while pages within a spread retain 12 pixels. Full-page views also retain an outline/shadow.
- Black bookmark flags are displayed larger at the physical PDF page's top-right corner. The persisted note geometry, sync and exports are unchanged; normal notes retain their existing rendering. When the corner lies off-screen or outside the crop, the centered bookmarked page gets a passive top-right viewport flag. Legacy stored note rectangles do not leave invisible document-selection targets; bookmarks remain editable in the sidebar and toggle through the viewport shortcut.
- A large translucent top-center arrow announces that the next Jumped SmartScroll step crosses to another PDF page. It stays hidden during Continuous/overview, before another column, when the entire page fits and at the document's end. It has no hitbox and cannot cover the quickbar's input.

Tests exercise actual pen taps, EPUB frame geometry/appearance, crop shadows, viewport indicators, page fitting, columns/spreads and existing input behavior. Native regressions cover navigation ownership, hardware key pairing, pen presets and appearance persistence. Physical Galaxy Tab S4/BOOX reproduction, firmware gestures and live-account sync were not available; these are not claimed as tested. Pinned PDF.js/runtime files remain unchanged.
